## 1. System Overview

**Cambus Connect Route Optimizer** is a multi-tenant, shift-isolated transportation decision engine built for college campus bus operations. It solves the core problem:

> *"When should buses merge, when should they stay separate, and how does tomorrow's schedule affect today's evening dispatch?"*
